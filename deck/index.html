The big idea

No full price.
Ever.

Minnesota's premier anonymous cheapskate squeezes every penny out of the Twin Cities (and beyond) so you do not have to. Saving money becomes a game you win with a friend who is slightly too proud of it.

The character

Breakfast sandwich head.
Gold chains.
Zero full-price items.

  • Vintage Minnesota layers: plaid flannel, a ball cap, coupons in hand.
  • Dollar signs, price tags and a cockatiel sidekick in every scene.
  • The Twin Cities skyline behind him, so it always feels local.
  • Anonymous by design. No names, no employer, no tells.

The voice

Dry. Deadpan.
One real tip
in every joke.

He says: "The second cup is for later. Put it in the fridge. Cold coffee is a personality, not a problem."

He says: "Go Sunday, not Saturday. Day two is when firm prices get negotiable and the owl starts looking like a bargain."

The joke gets the click. The tip earns the return visit.

Who it is for

Three kinds of
deal hunters

The Thrifter

Estate sales, bin stores, fill-a-bag days. Wants to know where to go and when.

The Coupon Stacker

Apps, game pieces, rewards. Wants the trick that saves the next five dollars.

The Fare Chaser

Error fares and cheap flights. Wants a heads up before the deal disappears.

What they share: they enjoy the hunt almost as much as the savings.

Modules 1 to 6

Receipt Roast

Type what you bought and what you paid. TC roasts it and shows what you should have paid.

Estate Sale Bingo

A five by five card of estate sale sightings. Tap to mark, then BINGO.

Price Check Slot Machine

Pull the lever. Three reels land on an item, a store and a price. STEAL or SKIP.

Thrift Horoscope

Pick your sign for this week's thrift fortune, lucky aisle and lucky price.

Haggle Simulator

Three rounds of haggling with a stubborn estate sale seller. Win or walk.

Thrift Fit Builder

Build an outfit out of thrift finds.
